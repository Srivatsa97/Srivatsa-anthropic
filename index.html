Inside Commvault

My contribution: game days at Commvault

Pick a repeated workflow. Build a working AI-assisted version with available tools. Present it to the team. Help everyone adopt it. Three I have designed and run:

01 RFP Response Acceleration
  • ProblemRFP responses took 3 to 4 hours each, were highly repetitive, inconsistent quality across the team.
  • BuiltMicrosoft Copilot plus an internal AI bot, with a prompt library covering 20 common RFP categories and our standard output structure.
  • ResultResponse time cut ~60%. Now the standard starting point across the India presales org.
02 ISE Daily Workflow Assistant
  • ProblemInside Sales Engineers losing hours to repetitive daily tasks: email drafting, follow-ups, meeting prep, technical summaries.
  • BuiltA prompt-based assistant on internal AI tools, each prompt written for the actual Commvault presales context.
  • ResultMeasurable time savings and higher-quality customer communications, with a shared library for the whole ISE team.
03 Clumio AWS Workload Recovery at Scale
  • ProblemEnterprise customers had AWS workloads backed up but had never tested recovery at scale. RPO and RTO targets unvalidated.
  • RanA hands-on game day: participants executed mass recovery across EC2, RDS, and S3. I designed the flow, guided the teams, ran the debrief.
  • ResultParticipants validated real RTO against targets and found backup-coverage gaps. Multiple accounts accelerated their Clumio deployment as a result.
