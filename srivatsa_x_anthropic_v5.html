How I am changing things internally at Commvault

Enterprise AI game days are where I excel at Commvault. Pick a repeated workflow. Build a working AI-assisted version with available tools. Present it to the team. Help everyone adopt it. These are three game days I have designed and run.

Game Day 01 RFP Response Acceleration

RFP responses were taking 3 to 4 hours per document. High repetition across similar questions. Quality inconsistent across team members.

Microsoft Copilot and an internal AI bot, structured with a prompt library covering 20 common RFP categories. Customer context injection and a section-by-section output format matching our standard response structure.

Ran a live demo for the presales team. Built a shared prompt library. Response time cut by approximately 60%. Now the standard starting point across the India presales org.

Game Day 02 ISE Daily Workflow Assistant

Inside Sales Engineers were spending significant time on repetitive daily tasks: email drafting, customer follow-ups, meeting prep, and technical summary write-ups.

A structured prompt-based assistant using internal AI tools designed around the most common ISE daily tasks. Each prompt written specifically for the Commvault presales context.

Demonstrated each prompt live. Built a shared library accessible to the full ISE team. Measurable time savings with teammates reporting higher quality output on customer communications.

Game Day 03 Clumio AWS Workload Recovery at Scale

Enterprise customers had AWS workloads backed up with Clumio but had never tested recovery at scale. Theoretical RPO and RTO targets had never been validated under real conditions.

A structured Clumio game day where participants executed mass recovery of AWS workloads hands-on across EC2, RDS, and S3 environments. Designed the session flow, guided teams through the recovery process, and facilitated the debrief.

Participants validated their actual RTO against stated targets and identified specific gaps in backup coverage. Multiple accounts accelerated their Clumio deployment directly as a result.
